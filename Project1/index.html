my Tunes

Your music, movies , and TV shows take center stage.

Welcome to My Tune – your space for music lovers. Music has the power to touch hearts, inspire creativity, and bring people together. At My Tune, we celebrate every beat, rhythm, and melody by sharing music that connects with your soul. Whether you enjoy calm tunes, energetic beats, or timeless classics, My Tune is here to be your companion on every mood and moment of life.

Music

Music has always been a universal language that speaks to the heart without the need for words. At My Tune, we believe that every note carries a story, every beat holds an emotion, and every melody connects us to memories we cherish. This section is dedicated to exploring the beauty of music in all its forms — from soulful acoustic tracks that calm the mind, to powerful rhythms that fill you with energy, to timeless classics that never fade. Whether you are looking for music to relax after a long day, to spark creativity while you work, or to lift your mood when you need motivation, My Tune brings you closer to the tunes that truly resonate with your life. Our goal is to create a space where music lovers can feel inspired, connected, and understood, because music is not just entertainment — it's a part of who we are.

Orange Music is available in myTune, and for iOS and Android devices.

The movie and TV collection you always wished for. Granted.

Music and movies are two powerful forms of art that bring emotions, stories, and imagination to life. At My Tune, we celebrate the magic of both worlds by exploring how music and cinema connect with our hearts and minds. Music gives us rhythm, energy, and feelings that words cannot express, while movies create stories that let us dream, escape, and experience different lives. Together, they shape our moods, inspire creativity, and build unforgettable memories. This section is designed for those who love the soulful beats of music and the storytelling magic of movies, offering a place where sound and visuals come together in harmony.

A world of entertainment. Available wherever you are.

Videos bring stories, ideas, and creativity to life in the most engaging way. At My Tune, our video section is designed to entertain, inspire, and connect with audiences who love visual storytelling. From music videos that blend rhythm with powerful imagery, to short films and creative clips that spark emotions, this space is all about capturing moments that leave a lasting impact. Whether you want to enjoy trending videos, explore artistic content, or simply relax with something fun to watch, the My Tune video section is here to keep you entertained and inspired every day.
